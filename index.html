Business letters

I draft the letters that keep accounts, store space, and listings moving. Names and figures are left out here.

Letter of intent
Introduces the company to a prospective consignment partner, lists current retail clients, and invites a conversation about terms.
Ingress request
Asks a retailer’s procurement head for consent to bring items into specific branches, with branch codes and an approval block.
Store visit request
Requests approval to visit a branch, meet the branch head, and check display locations, with dates and visiting personnel listed.
Rebate statement
Presents a year of sales and collection by branch, with the computed sell-in rebate and how it is offset against the next payable.
Display area requests
Requests additional or reinstated store space, including measured area breakdowns, to be added to an existing contract.
SKU retention request
Responds to a retailer’s planned SKU deactivation with reasons to keep selected items active.
Branch closure notice
Formally notifies a retail partner of a branch closure, with the reason stated clearly and politely.
